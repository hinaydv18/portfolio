How I work

Documented research,
not intuition.

Before applying a method, I check that it is the right one for the data in front of me. This principle runs through all my projects and becomes systematic whenever a technical choice has to be justified to a client or a team.

03

Quality, robustness, time, reproducibility, compliance.

05

Decision: Spearman was chosen for the correlation — since no variable is normally distributed, Pearson underestimated the stock/sales relationship. For segmentation, K-Means was preferred despite a lower score: its three balanced groups can be used directly by a purchasing team, whereas DBSCAN only isolates extreme cases. Every test was run on real data, with full traceability (versioned dataset, SHA-256 fingerprint, notebook annotated cell by cell).

BottleNeck — cleaning and making a 714-product catalogue reliable

Optimise management and clean the stock data of an online wine and spirits shop.

Context / business need

BottleNeck consolidates three exports (ERP, website, mapping table) to manage its catalogue, but the raw data contains inconsistencies (negative prices and stocks, duplicates) that distort commercial decisions.

Data
  • 3 source exports: ERP (825 rows), website (1,513 rows), mapping table (825 rows)
  • Final consolidated dataset: 714 products × 26 columns, across 6 families (wine is 92% of the catalogue)
Approach
  • Merging sources, correcting negative values, detecting outliers (Z-score)
  • Formal quality audit (% missing, duplicates, distributions)
  • Documented comparative research on two fronts: correlation method, then segmentation algorithm
  • Dataset versioning (raw → cleaned_v1) with a SHA-256 fingerprint for traceability
Results & impact
Rank correlation (Spearman) overturned an incorrect conclusion: stock and sales are in fact strongly correlated (0.759 versus 0.439 as estimated by Pearson). The chosen segmentation isolates 28 “high-end dormant” products to monitor first, to avoid tied-up capital.

Limitations: a snapshot at a single point in time, with no time dimension — a next iteration would benefit from including several periods to track how segments evolve.

Sanitoral — a portfolio tracking dashboard for IT & Marketing projects

Delivered as a Data Analyst for ESN DATA, for the client Sanitoral — scoped by Sophie, project manager in the Project Management Office.

Context / business need

Sanitoral runs IT projects (6 phases) and Marketing projects (4 phases) in parallel, with no consolidated view of their progress. The goal: assess the performance of each project and give three decision-making profiles — General Manager, Regional Director, Country Director — a view suited to their scope.

Data
  • Progress tracking of IT and Marketing projects, by phase, region and country
Approach
  • Data modelling and building visuals suited to each reading level (global, regional, country — with a world map for the Country Director)
  • Setting up Row Level Security so that each profile only accesses data within its scope — diagnosing and resolving the access issues met during configuration
Results & impact
A single dashboard where the General Manager monitors overall performance gaps to decide whether to stop or continue a project, while the Regional Director and the Country Director get alerts and corrective indicators at their own level — with no access to data outside their scope.

Limitations: this project needs precise metrics on the time savings observed since go-live.

Paris property market — valuing a portfolio and spotting opportunities

Analyse real estate price trends with Python to support a client's change of strategy.

Context / business need

A client wanted to understand property price dynamics in Paris in order to redirect their investment strategy and identify future opportunities.

Data
  • Property listings covering all 20 Paris arrondissements (price, postcode, property type)
Approach
  • Exploratory analysis by arrondissement, encoding of categorical variables (postcode, property type)
  • Linear regression model for valuation, applied to a portfolio of properties to be appraised
  • K-Means segmentation (2 clusters) to tell homes from commercial premises by price per m²
Results & impact
Valuation model with an R² ≈ 0.98 on the test set, and a clear separation between residential and commercial properties, which can be used to prioritise the client's portfolio.

Limitations: an R² this high calls for a data-leakage check before going into production; the model still needs validating on a fully external dataset.

Wine shop — managing margin, stock and promotions in one place

Build a dynamic Power BI dashboard and optimise the commercial performance of a wine shop.

Context / business need

A wine and spirits shop needed a single tool to track margins, stock turnover and promotion effectiveness, which until then were scattered across several files.

Data

UOI Games — identifying market segments before a first AAA title

Analyse a business request and identify the most relevant market segments for a studio preparing its first AAA game.

Context / business need

UOI Games was considering its first AAA game (RPG/FPS) and needed to assess the opportunity objectively: which genre, which platform, which positioning, before committing the budget.

Data
  • 3 cross-referenced datasets: video game sales, console sales, publisher data
  • Original survey (10+ questions, ~3 min, anonymous) conducted for market segmentation
Approach
  • PESTEL/SWOT analysis of the market before the quantitative analysis
  • Benchmark of top RPG/FPS titles, correlation tests (Pearson) on sales
  • Launch platform recommendation, delivered as an Excel workbook with dynamic formulas, a 19-slide deck and a Word report
Results & impact
Recommendation to prioritise PS5, Xbox and PC before the Switch for a first AAA RPG/FPS, backed by the benchmark and the segmentation survey results.

Limitations: the survey relies on self-reported answers and a sample not guaranteed to be representative; the correlation tests would now benefit from a Spearman reading alongside, as done since on the BottleNeck project.
